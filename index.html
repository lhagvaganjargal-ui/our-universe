Our Story

Our story began during the autumn harvest festival. I never expected that a simple day of harvesting would turn into something so special and meaningful. That was the day I met you. You came over to me and offered to help, and little did I know that this small moment would become the beginning of our beautiful story. From that day on, things slowly started to change. We began spending more time together, sharing little moments, making memories, laughing, and getting to know each other better. What started as a simple meeting turned into something I never knew I needed. And now, here we are, spending almost every day together and creating countless memories along the way. Sometimes I think about how lucky I am that you decided to come over and help me that day. If you hadn't, who knows when or how we would have met? It's funny how the smallest moments can lead to the most beautiful things in life. I wouldn't change the way our story began because it brought me to you. And if I could go back to that autumn day, knowing everything I know now, I would still choose to meet you all over again. ❤️
